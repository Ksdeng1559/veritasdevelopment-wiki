# Prime Lee's Summit — Pitch Summary

**For:** Prospective capital partners and JV collaborators
**Status:** Working draft — to be refined based on sponsor confirmation

## The Opportunity

A 19.05-acre parcel on 291 Highway in **Lee's Summit, Missouri** — the **#1 multifamily submarket in the Kansas City metro** — at $3.5M list price ($184K/acre).

## Why Lee's Summit

- Top multifamily submarket in KC metro (per Legendary Apartment Brokers)
- Proximity to Kansas City + major highway access
- Robust local economy, low crime, strong job market
- 174 new-construction communities in the area
- Rent growth +6.4% YTD in Jackson County
- Costco-anchored East Village ($481M, Drake Development) advancing
- 6+ active multifamily/apartment builds (see [`../../market/lees-summit/btr/six-rental-builds-pipeline.md`](../../market/lees-summit/btr/six-rental-builds-pipeline.md))

## Why Veritas

> *"Built on Integrity. Driven by Excellence."*

- **Founded by David Poole** — 22+ years construction + development experience, 13+ years senior development leadership
- **3-person leadership team** with complementary skills (construction, concrete, real estate)
- **7 disciplines, 1 team** — full-scope coordination reduces need to manage multiple contractors
- **8 completed multifamily projects** demonstrated capability
- **Local expertise** — Daniel Bailey born/raised in Lee's Summit; David Poole's Kansas City metro network

## Risks & Mitigants

| Risk | Mitigant |
|---|---|
| Speculative land (currently unentitled) | Joint-venture with landowner; phased approach; early entitlement as de-risking milestone |
| Rising community friction pattern (13 protest petitions across LS in 2026) | Proactive community engagement plan; traffic/infrastructure mitigation built into design |
| Capital cost (rate environment) | Staged capital model preserves optionality |
| Construction cost inflation | Veritas site-prep + concrete expertise locks down first 30% of project cost |
