# Protest Petitions & Community Friction

> Tracking organized community opposition to development in Lee's Summit / Jackson County — a real signal for any new project.

## Why It Matters

In 2026, a **Senior Community CCRC** in Lee's Summit received **13 protest petitions** during its approval process — an unprecedented level of organized community friction. Combined with traffic concerns and infrastructure-delay criticisms across multiple projects, this signals a new normal for entitlement strategy.

## Pattern Observed

| Project | Friction |
|---|---|
| Senior Community CCRC (172u) | 13 protest petitions, site revisions required |
| Costco neighbors (Oldham Village East) | Traffic / safety / flooding concerns |
| Soccer dome (separate) | Public controversy |
| Road projects | Citizen demand for completion timelines |

## Implications for Veritas Prime Lee's Summit

1. **Build community engagement into entitlement strategy** — not a final step
2. **Identify and engage with buffer-zone neighbors early** — 185-ft buffer pattern observed
3. **Address infrastructure head-on** — traffic mitigation, drainage, connectivity
4. **Use the BTR anchor narrative** — community sentiment is more positive when retail/amenity is included
