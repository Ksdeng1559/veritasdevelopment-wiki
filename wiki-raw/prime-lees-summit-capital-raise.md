# Prime Lee's Summit — Institutional JV Capital Raise Plan

## Purpose
Second-stage capital raise for Prime Lee's Summit, following the simpler Stonehaven Estates residential raise. Prime is an **institutional-scale mixed-use development capitalization**, not a single undifferentiated equity request.

> **Core strategy:** Capitalize Prime in stages and, where useful, by asset component. Separate predevelopment/entitlement risk, horizontal infrastructure, multifamily construction, retail construction, and permanent financing so each capital source is matched to the risk it is best suited to underwrite.

## Project Concept
- **Sponsor:** Veritas Development Group LLC — David Poole
- **Location:** Highway 291, Lee's Summit, Missouri
- **Type:** Grocery-anchored mixed-use development
- 5 × 5-storey multifamily buildings, ~610 residents
- Price Chopper grocery anchor
- Two retail strip/plaza components (~16 stores)
- CVS/pharmacy component, restaurant/pub component
- Community gym and resort-style pool
- ~800+ parking spaces, 25+ EV charging stalls
- Highway 291 frontage, ~33,000 cars/day

> **Diligence note:** National/regional tenant names on concept renderings must NOT be represented to investors as committed tenants unless Veritas can produce executed leases, LOIs, term sheets, or other supporting documentation.

## Why Prime is the Second Raise
Stonehaven = straightforward residential subdivision underwriting (land, horizontal development, lot sales/vertical construction). Prime is more complex: value created through multiple stages:
1. Site control and entitlement
2. Anchor-tenant and leasing progress
3. Horizontal infrastructure
4. Multifamily construction
5. Retail construction
6. Lease-up and stabilization
7. Refinance and/or component disposition

## Recommended Legal / Investment Architecture
Master development entity with separate component-level entities:
- Prime Master Development LLC → Multifamily Parcel LLC, Grocery/Retail Center LLC, Pharmacy/Pad LLC, Restaurant/Pad LLC, Common Infrastructure/Amenities
- Review by project counsel, tax advisors, lenders, securities counsel before implementation.

## Capital Stack Architecture (layered)
| Layer | Purpose | Likely Source |
|---|---|---|
| Predevelopment/Sponsor Equity | Site control, design, engineering, entitlement, legal, leasing | Sponsor equity, family office, high-risk capital |
| Land/Entitlement JV | Acquisition through shovel-ready | JV equity, preferred equity, land capital |
| Horizontal Infrastructure | Roads, utilities, stormwater, grading | Development lender, infrastructure capital, municipal tools |
| Multifamily Construction | Apartment construction + lease-up | Senior construction debt + multifamily JV equity |
| Retail Construction | Grocery/retail shell, TI, pads | Retail construction debt + retail equity/JV |
| Permanent Financing | Refinance stabilized components | Bank, life company, agency-eligible, CMBS/private |
