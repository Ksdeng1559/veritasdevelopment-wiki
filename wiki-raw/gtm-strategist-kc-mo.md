## Positioning
Veritas Development Group LLC (David Poole) is positioned as the **GTM strategist for land developers and investors in Kansas City / Lee's Summit, Missouri** — the firm that turns market signals into a repeatable revenue pipeline. Service-line positioning layered on the sponsor/developer identity.

## Core Problem
Deal flow in the KC corridor is episodic, not systematic. Landowners lack execution paths; investors lack documented mandates; builders' demand is never captured; developers depend on brokers and luck. Cost of status quo: missed corridors, dead equity, slow absorption.

## Five Revenue Motions
1. Landowner origination — planning records, parcel data, listings, succession events, infrastructure announcements
2. Investor mandate capture — actual capital criteria (check size, asset class, geography, return target, structure), not names
3. Builder demand capture — lot/geography/product/absorption requirements → lot-takedown commitments
4. Referral-channel development — brokers, engineers, attorneys, lenders, title, CPAs as recurring sources
5. Authority & proof — evidence-backed insights, readiness checklists, milestone updates

## Operating Loop
Market selection → discovery → signal verification → scoring → decision-maker resolution → research brief → outreach → discovery → assessment → proposal/JV evaluation → diligence → engagement/match → attribution

## 90-Day Targets
25 properties/owners · 20 verified mandates · 10 builder profiles · 25 referral partners (10 active) · 15 discovery calls · 3–5 paid assessments · 5 matches · weekly content
