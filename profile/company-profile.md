## Public brand narrative

Veritas Development Group LLC presents itself as a practical, execution-led development and construction organization. The publicly indexed website copy emphasizes "Groundwork done right the first time," positioning the business around dependable early-stage site work and construction readiness. Its founder, David Poole, is identified as bringing more than 20 years of construction experience.

That gives Veritas a credible foundation for a development message built on a simple promise: reduce risk at the beginning of a project by correctly planning, preparing and delivering the site work on which the rest of the development depends.

Before representing any additional in-house capabilities, confirm the precise scope, licensing, subcontractor model, geographic coverage, equipment ownership and insurance.

## Strategic development profile

In addition to the public service message, Veritas is developing a broader real-estate development thesis for current opportunities in the Kansas City metro:

- Identify property where planning, servicing, entitlement, phasing or construction coordination can create measurable value.
- Work with property owners as partners where a conventional sale does not best capture the value created.
- Use staged capital: seed/angel capital for early de-risking work, then project/JV equity and senior construction financing as conditions are met.
- Create transparent capital stacks and investor waterfalls aligned to project risk and contribution.

This strategic development profile is a working business model, not necessarily a statement published on the current website.

## Ideal client and partner segments

- Landowners with underutilized or development-ready property who want an alternative to an immediate sale.
- Residential developers/builders requiring dependable site preparation and parcel-readiness support.
- Commercial and mixed-use developers requiring early works, grading, drainage or building-pad execution.
- Municipal/infrastructure-adjacent projects where trenching, drainage and site access are material delivery requirements.
- Capital partners interested in clearly bounded early-stage development opportunities with defined milestones, diligence and downside analysis.

## Capital-raise application relevance

For the Angel Investor Capital Raise application, this company profile should populate the Sponsor section and drive the following evidence requests:
