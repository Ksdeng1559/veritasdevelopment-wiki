## What is Build-to-Rent (BTR)?

**BTR** = homes purpose-built and owned by a single investor/operator as long-term rentals (vs. Build-to-Sell, sold to buyers). The developer holds the asset, recovers cost + margin through rent, and exits by selling the stabilized portfolio to a REIT or institution 5–10 years later.

## Why it matters for Veritas

1. **Capital partner alignment** — Seth Wilson (Clarity Equity) actively searching KS/MO/OK/AR/TX for BTR
2. **Geographic fit** — KC metro = top-10 BTR market nationally
3. **Capability fit** — Veritas's site-prep + excavation depth = competitive moat for BTR operators who don't own site work
4. **Pipeline overlap** — 6 Lee's Summit rental builds maps directly to BTR-investor criteria
5. **Demand drivers** — millennials + boomers both want renting flexibility
