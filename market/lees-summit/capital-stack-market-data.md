## 3. Public Incentive Stack (Gap Financing Tools)

| Tool | Min Investment | Cap/Level | Duration | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TIF** | $5M new / $3M existing | 25% of private dev costs | Up to 23yr (33yr multi-area) | Captures 100% incremental property tax (PILOTs) + 50% incremental sales/utility tax (EATs) |
| **Chapter 100 (IRB)** | $5M new / $3M existing | 50% base | 10yr (5yr equipment) | City takes title during bond term; property tax abatement + 100% sales tax exemption on construction materials |
| **Chapter 353 (Urban Redev.)** | $5M new / $3M existing | 50% base (up to 75%/10yr + 37.5%/15yr per city doc) | 10yr (statutory max 25yr) | Targets blight clearance/rehab |
| **LCRA** | $500K | 50% (up to 100% on incremental value) | 10yr (statutory max 25yr) | Land clearance for redevelopment |
| **Sales Tax Reimbursement** | $250K (public improvements) | Up to 1/2 cent | 10yr | Pledges new sales tax to fund infra |
| **CID** | Public improvement required | Up to 1 cent | Per agreement | Special assessments/property tax/sales tax |
| **TDD** | Public improvement required | Up to 1 cent | Per agreement | Funds transit/road infra |
| **NID** | Public improvement required | Up to 1% per $100 valuation | Max 20yr | GO bonds repaid via special assessment |

**Key qualifying criteria:**
- Company equity requirement: **≥15% of total project costs** from equity (not bank loans) for favorable consideration
- "But For Test" — must prove project infeasible without incentive, or delivers substantially enhanced public benefit
- Job creation measured over 24 months; wage thresholds tied to Jackson County Average Wage (CAW) — sliding scale up to 100% abatement + 5yr extension for 25+ jobs at 190% CAW
- General Fund/annual appropriation **will not be pledged** to back incentives
- No retrospective incentives — must disclose intent to seek incentives before any permits issued
- Reimbursement ratio (incentive $ / total project cost incl. land) reviewed against city's historical approval pattern

## 5. Flag: Fit vs. Known Target Investor (Clarity Equity Fund)

Per `investors/clarity-equity-fund/investor-profile.md`, Clarity Equity Real Estate Fund explicitly:
- **Avoids speculative land acquisition**
- Targets only **shovel-ready** or **value-add with established income streams**
- Requires min 2.0x gross equity multiple, semi-liquid structure, KS/MO/OK/AR/TX geography (fits), office→multifamily / value-add apartments / core plus focus

The Land Reserve North project is **raw, undeveloped land** with no finalized infrastructure cost or entitlement completion — this is the profile Clarity says it avoids. If Clarity is the target LP, the capital stack pitch likely needs to lean on the **public incentive stack (TIF/Ch.100/Ch.353) to de-risk the land/horizontal-infra layer** before positioning any private equity ask, or reposition around a shovel-ready sub-phase (e.g. Phase 1, 306 ac, already zoned) rather than the full 1,036-acre raw parcel.
