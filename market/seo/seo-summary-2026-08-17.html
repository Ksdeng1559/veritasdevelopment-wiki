Critical — but entirely fixable

Veritas has a real website but it's basically invisible to search engines. The root cause is that the site is a JavaScript-rendered single-page application (SPA), which means search engines see a near-empty page. All 4 issues below can be fixed in 1-2 hours with a small code change or by switching the homepage to a server-rendered or pre-rendered template.

4 Issues Found

Warning missing-h1 Missing H1 heading
Add a single H1 that states the page's main topic, consistent with the title tag.
Warning no-outgoing-links Page has no outgoing links
Add links to related pages, the parent category, or the homepage. If the page's navigation is rendered by JavaScript, make sure it also exists in the server-rendered HTML.
Warning thin-content Thin content
Either expand the page with genuinely useful content, noindex it, or consolidate it into a stronger page. If the content exists but is rendered by JavaScript, ensure it is server-rendered or pre-rendered.
Info meta-description-too-short Meta description too short
Expand the description to roughly 70–160 characters that summarize the page and give a reason to click.

"How long until we actually rank on Google?"

Honest answer: 3-6 months for Tier 1 keywords (Multifamily, Concrete Services) to reach page 1-2; 6-9 months for Tier 2; 9-12 months for Tier 3. This assumes:

  • Technical fixes ship in week 1
  • Tier 1 pages published in week 1-2
  • 5-10 backlinks acquired in month 1-2
  • Google re-crawls and re-indexes the site (typically 2-4 weeks after fixes)

Realistically, you'll see movement (page 5-10) within 6-8 weeks of publishing Tier 1 content. Page 1-2 ranking for the highest-volume keywords takes longer because of competition (Zillow, Realtor.com, established local agencies).

"How many leads per month will this generate?"

Hard to predict exactly because conversion depends on page copy, calls-to-action, and lead nurturing. Industry benchmark for B2B services (contractors, developers) is:

  • 1-3% of organic search traffic converts to a lead (form fill or phone call)
  • If you rank for 1,000 monthly searches and get 5% click-through-rate (CTR) → 50 visitors/month
  • 1-3% of 50 = 0.5-1.5 leads/month from organic alone

By month 6-9, with 6 cluster pages ranking, this can grow to 3-10 leads/month from organic. Combined with paid + referral, this becomes a meaningful channel.

"Why isn't Veritas already ranking? We've been in business 22+ years."

Three reasons, in order of impact:

  1. The site is JavaScript-rendered — Google sees a near-empty page. This is the biggest issue (fixable in 1-2 hours with a code change).
  2. No backlinks — Without inbound links from other sites, Google has no signal that Veritas is an authoritative source.
  3. No optimized content for the keywords people search — Your homepage talks about Veritas; it doesn't answer "concrete contractor in Lee's Summit."

None of these are structural problems. They're all fixable in 1-2 weeks of focused work. Your business credibility (22+ years, 8 multifamily projects, 7 disciplines) is the asset; we just need to make it visible to Google.

"What happens if we don't do this?"

Honestly, the status quo is fine short-term because:

  • Most Veritas business comes from referrals, repeat clients, and existing relationships
  • Your existing client pipeline is strong enough to maintain revenue

But long-term:

  • Local agencies (ReeceNichols, RE/MAX, etc.) are ranking for "lees summit real estate" searches — your customers are landing on their sites, not yours
  • New developers entering the KC market (Drake Development, Griffin Riley, etc) will eventually optimize their sites and out-rank you
  • The 6 rental builds in Lee's Summit's pipeline (Oldham Village East, Legacy Ridge, Arborside, etc) represent your market — and you don't appear for any of the search terms those projects would generate

The opportunity cost of doing nothing compounds. Every month the site stays invisible is a month of leads going to competitors who happened to optimize first.
