## The KC Value Proposition

> *"Kansas City has evolved from a 'hidden gem' into one of the premier destinations for institutional and private capital in the United States."*

The 3-bullet competitive case:

| # | Value lever | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Competitive Cap Rates (6.36-7.44%)** | Higher yield than coastal premium |
| 2 | **Insulated from Sun Belt "Oversupply"** | Conservative 3.3% under-construction pipeline |

## Veritas Positioning within KC Capital Flows

### The Sleeper-City Thesis

KC is at an inflection point — institutional capital is moving from coastal/Sun Belt to KC. Lee's Summit is **one of the most under-priced cities in the metro** for new development (compared to Overland Park, Brookside, etc.).

### 3 Strategic Plays for Veritas

**Play 1:** Ground-up mixed-use in Lee's Summit target areas (Douglas/Tudor, US 50, South M-291)
- Use the **cost advantage** (94.1 composite vs national 100) to underprice competitors
- Position for **Cap rate compression** as institutional capital keeps flowing in

**Play 2:** Industrial site-prep for the 3.68M sq ft Land Reserve NLS
- Direct fit for Veritas's site-prep + excavation depth
- Panasonic-block-scale deals need the Veritas capability stack

**Play 3:** BTR partnerships near 2026 World Cup venue
- Capitalize on the **institutional flow** toward BTR
- Partner with operators like Avital Homes
- Use the **20% institutional SFR concentration** as validation

## Risks

1. **Cap rate compression risk** — institutional flows could narrow Veritas's window
2. **Interest rate environment** — affects feasibility
3. **General macro slowdown** — KC insulated but not immune
4. **Competition from institutional GCs** — well-capitalized firms entering KC
