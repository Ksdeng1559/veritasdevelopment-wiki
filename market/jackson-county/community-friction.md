| Friction type | Activity observed |
|---|---|
| **Protest petitions** | 13 on Senior Community CCRC — site revisions required |
| **Traffic concerns** | Costco neighbors (Oldham Village East) — traffic/safety/flooding |
| **Infrastructure delays** | Citizen demand for completion timelines |
| **Public controversies** | Soccer dome project |

## Why This Matters

For any new development (including Veritas Prime Lee's Summit), organized community opposition can:
- Delay entitlements
- Force re-designs
- Increase project costs
- Require additional community engagement budget

## Mitigants to Build In

1. **Pre-application community engagement** — meet buffer-zone neighbors (185-ft radius) before formal submittal
2. **Traffic study + mitigation plan** — particularly for highway-adjacent sites
3. **Drainage + flood modeling** — old concern, new salience after recent weather events
4. **Anchor retail narrative** — community sentiment flips positive when Costco/grocery is included
5. **Phased construction** — limits peak impact, builds community trust over time
