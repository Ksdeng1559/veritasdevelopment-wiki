## 6. Investor Demographic Storylines

### For apartment deals
- 26.6% renter share + stable employment → low leasing risk + high retention
- $102K median HH income → supports market-rate rents
- Working-age demographic (median 38.8) → consistent household formation

### For single-family / BTR
- 73.4% current homeownership (high) + 48.8% Bachelor's+ → qualified buyer pool
- Low poverty, strong income → manageable affordability stress
- Family-oriented (69.6% family households, 25.5% under 18) → "missing middle" demand

### For retail / commercial
- Stable households + predictable commute → consistent neighborhood spend
- High broadband + computer adoption → supports modern retail/service formats
- 17,937-student R-7 district → consistent family consumption patterns
