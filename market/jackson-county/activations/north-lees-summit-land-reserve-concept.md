## Strategic Implications

### Veritas Site-Prep Opportunity

This is the **single largest near-term site-prep opportunity in Lee's Summit**:
- 3,451 residential units across 3 phases
- 3.68M sq ft of light industrial
- 1.6M sq ft of retail
- 302K sq ft of medical/office

### Phase 1 Sequencing (Earliest Work)

**Phase 1 (306 acres):**
- 133 ac commercial retail (foundational)
- 72 ac light industrial
- 80 ac R-2 residential
- 11 ac R-1
- 10 ac senior housing
- 740 residential units + 249 senior housing units
- 1.84M sq ft of building space

### Phase 2 Sequencing (Mid-term)

**Phase 2 (544 acres):**
- 217 ac light industrial (massive addition)
- 206 ac R-1 residential (huge low-density push)
- 40 ac mixed-use
- 68 ac park
- 13 ac R-2 residential
- 932 residential units + 20 ac park

### Phase 3 Sequencing (Long-term)

**Phase 3 (186 acres):**
- 88 ac Town Center
- 69 ac mixed-use
- 20 ac park
- 9 ac commercial retail
- 1,779 residential units (highest density per acre)

## Veritas Decision Framework

| If we... | Then we... |
|---|---|
| Land positioned in Phase 1 (306 ac) | Compete for site-prep + grading contracts (2026-2028) |
| Land positioned in Phase 2 (544 ac) | Mid-tier contractor role (2028-2032) |
| Land positioned in Phase 3 (186 ac) | Long-term JV partner role (2032-2040) |
| Want to influence the plan | Engage LSEDC + Planning now |

## Path to Engagement

1. **Identify the developer/owner** of the 1,036-acre Land Reserve (likely a church entity or land banker)
2. **Confirm phasing timeline** with Lee's Summit Planning Department
3. **Pre-application consultation** with LSEDC for incentive-stacking
4. **Form JV with a BTR operator** if we're pursuing the residential phases
5. **Identify industrial partners** for the 3.68M sq ft light industrial
