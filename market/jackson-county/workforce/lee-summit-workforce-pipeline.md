## The Talent Pool

### Geographic Reach
- **905,800 workers** within a 45-minute drive of Lee's Summit
- **80% of workers** commute fewer than 24 miles to work
- 17,937 students in Lee's Summit R-7 School District (one of the largest in MO side of KC metro)

### Why This Matters for Developers
- Stable, predictable labor force for construction + operations
- Short commute distances = high worker retention
- Quality workforce = supports premium positioning

## The Education Ecosystem

Lee's Summit runs a **uniquely integrated education-to-workforce pipeline** that few US cities can replicate:

### Pipeline Components

| Stage | Institution | Specialty |
|---|---|---|
| K-12 | **Lee's Summit R-7 School District** | 22:1 student/teacher ratio, 80% staff with Bachelor's+, all 3 LSHS in top 7% of MO |
| HS Junior/Senior | **Summit Technology Academy (STA)** | Engineering, CS, Health Science, Human Services, Finance, Arts & Communications |
| Dual-enrollment | **Missouri Innovation Campus (MIC)** | University of Central Missouri + MCC + R-7 partnership; HS students graduate as college juniors |
| Associate | **Metropolitan Community College (MCC-Longview)** | 8 program areas, professional orgs, employer engagement |
| Bachelor's | **University of Central Missouri (UCM)** | 13,734 students, 150+ programs, Lee's Summit campus |

### STA Signature Programs
- Missouri Innovation Campus
- KU Degree in 3
- Cerner Scholars
- IBCP (International Baccalaureate Career Programme)
- UCM-STA Fast Track to BS in Nursing

### Education Recognition
- **2016-2017:** STA honored as Exemplar School by The Partnership for 21st Century Learning
- 2017: Recognition by former President Barack Obama
- 2017: MIC grand opening of $10M+ facility with industry-grade equipment
- Students pooled from **44 area high schools**
