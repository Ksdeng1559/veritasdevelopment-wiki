Statutory Financial Benefits & Tax Incentives for Developers

Lee’s Summit utilizes structured statutory programs authorized under Missouri law to reduce upfront infrastructure costs for qualifying corporate and residential projects.

Property Tax Abatements & Shielding

Chapter 353 Redevelopment Incentives

: Offers statutory property tax relief specifically designed to clear blighted or underutilized zones. Eligible developers receive

up to 75% tax abatement over the first 10 years

, and up to

37.5% abatement over the subsequent 15 years

Chapter 100 Industrial Revenue Bonds (IRB)

: Allows the city to take structural title of a facility during development, rendering the construction phase

100% exempt from real estate and personal sales tax

on building materials.

Specialized Taxing Districts & Infrastructure Subsidies

Tax Increment Financing (TIF)

: Captures the incremental increase in property and local sales taxes generated by a new development over a maximum of

to directly reimburse developers for grading, utility installation, and public road widening.

Community Improvement Districts (CID)

: Permits developers to levy an additional

0.5% to 1.0% local sales tax

strictly within the property boundary to directly fund ongoing security, landscaping, and private parking structure maintenance.

Transportation Development Districts (TDD)

: Enables dedicated funding loops to subsidize structural traffic interchanges, signalization, and transit turnouts required by mega-scale projects.

Public Sentiment Profile & Civic Friction Points

Public sentiment mining indicates a highly positive baseline toward master planning, though friction surfaces regarding dense residential zoning and municipal execution pacing.

Primary Growth Catalysts (Positive Sentiment)

The "Costco Effect"

: The relocation and opening of the new Lee's Summit Costco Warehouse at the SE corner of US 50 and MO 291 represents the primary narrative driving localized civic excitement. Residents strongly favor retail options that prevent leaking sales tax revenue to neighboring jurisdictions.

Premium Visual Assets

: Highly visible progress updates (such as developer reels on Instagram and grading tracking videos on Facebook) for Discovery Park and Oldham Village maintain high public alignment with corporate growth.

Strategic Friction Points & Resistance (Negative Sentiment)

Developer Incentive Scrutiny

: High-value tax abatements (such as the modified $408M mass timber initiative) draw systematic critiques regarding public subsidy allocation and the structural shifting of tax burdens away from corporate developers.

Density vs. Infrastructure Strain

: Residential expansions, specifically high-density senior housing complexes and rental properties, meet localized resistance via formal protest petitions. Civic worry centers on traffic bottlenecks along key arteries and overcrowding in the R-7 school district.

Key Recommendations for Developers

Lead with Commercial Anchors

: Local authorities and residents strongly favor projects anchored by significant retail or corporate magnets that offset residential infrastructure demands.

Mitigate Traffic Impact Proactively

: Proposals leveraging specialized infrastructure funding mechanisms (like TDDs) to directly solve surrounding roadway constraints face a significantly smoother path through the city council.

Engage Early with the LSEDC

: Navigating localized Chapter 353 and TIF commissions requires close alignment with the city's economic development agency to secure timely, optimized project approvals.

Lee’s Summit is 16 miles southeast of downtown Kansas City and is one of the fastest-growing communities in the state of Missouri. With a current population of 108,000, the city offers companies and residents a thriving local economy, award-winning schools, nimble workforce and training programs, plentiful public and private investment, diverse housing stock and coveted community lifestyle amenities. At 65 square miles, the sixth-largest in the state, the city has room to grow with recently-annexed properties in strategic, highway-served locations. The ease of access to nearby highways provides Lee’s Summit with a broad workforce. Lee’s Summit has access to a pool of approximately 905,800 workers within a 45-minute drive. 80% of workers in Lee’s Summit drive fewer than 24 miles to work.

Greater Kansas City Foreign Trade Zone

Greater Kansas City Foreign Trade Zone

is a designated location in the United States that is outside of customs territory for purposes of collection of US Customs duties and fees. Foreign goods and merchandise are admitted duty-unpaid to a Foreign Trade Zone where they can be stored, processed, repackaged, transformed destroyed or otherwise handled by company employees providing value-added work before they leave the FTZ (when a formal entry is filed) or exported. FTZ users enjoy many cost-saving benefits in cost of goods stored, produced/assembled in FTZ’s as well as cost reductions in transportation, distribution and other components of their supply chains.

Lee’s Summit prides itself on its collaborative, community-based approach to education that has led to the creation of a unique Education EcoSystem unlike any other in the nation. Summit Technology Academy (STA) is an innovative, national education model that prepares high school juniors and seniors for high-demand, high-wage professions. Through a revolutionary partnership among the Lee’s Summit R-7 School District, Metropolitan Community College, the University of Central Missouri and business partners throughout the Greater Kansas City region, STA created the Missouri Innovation Campus Program (MIC). The MIC program is designed to revolutionize the way students learn and work by bridging the gap between skills and workforce demands. MIC gives industry leaders an opportunity to work hand-in-hand with the participating education partners to create internships and curriculum that ensure graduates are poised to enter the workforce with the skills necessary to make them valuable associates.

Students, who are pooled from 44 area high schools, are offered career-specific, hands-on training as well as dual credit in areas such as Engineering, Computer Science, Health Science, Human Services & Finance and Arts & Communication. Students who choose to pursue college are able to enter as a junior their first year out of high school with little-to-no debt. This Education EcoSystem is a mutually-beneficial investment for both businesses and students. Industry leaders are allowed a seat at the table when it comes to curriculum, supplying them with the highly-trained, qualified job candidates needed in today’s tight labor market. Students at MIC gain invaluable input from experienced professionals and in turn, are prepared to stand out in a competitive workforce. The MIC program’s unique career-driven education philosophy has made STA a national model for workforce-readiness. In the 2016-2017 school year, STA was honored as an Exemplar School by The Partnership for 21st Century Learning. STA has even gained recognition from former President Barack Obama. In 2017, MIC celebrated the grand opening of brand-new facilities, equipped with state-of-the-art, industry-grade equipment.

Target Areas of Development

Streets of West Pryor

Streets of West Pryor is a 70-acre mixed use development that will be located near I-470 and NW Pryor Rd. Construction on the $178 million project began in 2019. When it is complete, Streets of West Pryor will hold a 235 unit apartment complex, 160+ unit age-restricted apartments, 165+ room hotel, a 63,000 square foot grocery store and a broad range of retail and dining options. The design includes ample green space, as well as trails connecting to nearby Lowenstein Park. Parts of the development are expected to be complete by Summer 2020.

Paragon Star

Located near I-470 and View High Drive, Paragon Star will be a $250 million sports-complex anchored project. The sports complex will include 10 regulation soccer fields and is expected to be complete by Summer 2020. Plans for Paragon Star also include a commercial development, Paragon Star Village, which will include a 390-unit luxury multifamily complex, 100,000 square feet of Class A office space, two hotels, 90,000 square feet of retail and a 12,000 square foot entertainment venue.

New Longview

Located at 3rd St. and View High Rd., New Longview combines classic Kansas City architecture with the magnificent beauty of the R.A. Long estate. Transforming significant historical structures into usable community centers and landmarks is the signature of the community. Choices are abundant, with over 1,100 residences, 600,000 square feet of office and retail space, park land and modern conveniences inspired by traditional neighborhood design. In 2019 the Lee’s Summit City Council approved a Community Improvement District for the area. While development is still in the works, plans include a one acre event space that will host special events and public concerts. The addition is expected to break ground in 2020.

Summit Technology Campus

Summit Technology Campus (STC), 777 NW Blue Parkway, is located within one mile of Summit Woods Crossing and Summit Fair shopping centers. STC is an outstanding, large-scale office site ideally suited to accommodate office, call center, data center or light manufacturing operations. At just over one million square feet, STC offers ample space for most applications. AT&T originally developed the property as a microelectronics manufacturing facility. The resulting infrastructure has proven to be well-suited for data center and disaster recovery tenants. STC also houses the Lee’s Summit Integration Campus, a 500,000-square-foot tech hub, equipped with a dual grid substation that has full-capacity feeds capable of carrying an entire station load.

Lee’s Summit is the sixth-largest city in the state with 65 square miles of land, yet the city has plenty of room to grow. Recently-annexed properties in strategic locations are served by two interstates (I-470 and I-70) and two federal highways (U.S. 50 and U.S. 71). Lee’s Summit is accessible to 85 percent of U.S. markets within 2 days.

Lee’s Summit consistently ranks on Money’s Best Places to Live, thanks to a diverse array of housing options, award-winning K-12 school district and the pedestrian-friendly, historic Downtown. Downtown Lee’s Summit Main Street won a “Great American Main Street” award in 2010 by the National Trust for Historic Preservation. Residents enjoy access to a comprehensive park and trail system and Longview Lake recreation area. Lee’s Summit offers a unique balance of a close-knit community and nearby access to attractions in the Kansas City metropolitan area such as the Nelson-Atkins Museum of Art, Country Club Plaza, Kansas City Chiefs, Kansas City Royals, Kansas City Mavericks, Powell Gardens Botanical Gardens and more.

With 17,937 students, Lee’s Summit R-7 is one of the largest school districts on the Missouri side of the Kansas City metropolitan area. Its size allows the district to offer a variety of accelerated programs and extracurricular activities, as well as access to highly-trained, quality teachers. More than 80 percent of staff employed by the Lee’s Summit school district hold a degree higher than a Bachelor’s degree.

Additionally, professional staff in the Lee’s Summit school district have an average of 15.8 years of experience. LSR-7’s highly-qualified staff, combined with its 22-to-one student-to-teacher ratio, have helped the district gain national accolades and awards. Lee's Summit R-7's high schools continue to earn recognition in U.S. News & World Report's 'Best High Schools' rankings, with all three high schools placing in the top 7% of Missouri high schools for 2025-26."

Lee's SummitMunicipal Airport

Kansas City/Lee's Summit Regional Airport

(LXT) is located just southeast of Kansas City, MO. Its location makes LXT a convenient way for business leaders to connect to wherever their business takes them. In 2017, the airport expanded its runway to 5,101 feet. The Class C-II runway allows the Lee’s Summit Municipal Airport to accommodate corporate aircrafts up to 60,000 lbs. The new capability gives business leaders greater accessibility to the resources they need for their companies to thrive.

Kansas City/Lee's Summit Regional Airport

is a full-service Fixed Base Operator with concierge service to help you schedule reservations and coordinate service needs. LXT offers fueling and a pilot’s lounge. The airport also has 111 hangar units of various sizes available for lease. With its proximity to the Kansas City metropolitan area, Lee’s Summit Municipal Airport offers a convenient point of access to area attractions such as the Country Club Plaza, and the Power and Light District, while its size provides pilots and passengers the opportunity to avoid the crowd of larger airports.
