The Lee’s Summit Economic Development Council

(LSEDC) is a public-private partnership devoted to improving the economic well-being of Lee’s Summit, Missouri. With an economic base of more than $2 billion and growing, Lee’s Summit is the employment epicenter of Eastern Jackson County in the Kansas City metropolitan area.

The LSEDC leverages local, regional, state and national partnerships and collaborations to support economic development efforts to fulfill its objective of promoting and enhancing the City of Lee’s Summit as a model community for economic innovation fueled by a pro-business environment, diverse economic base and a commitment to strong relationships and work ethic.

Our Mission

The Lee’s Summit Economic Development Council is committed to fostering sustainable economic growth and prosperity in Lee’s Summit by attracting new businesses, marketing and promoting our community, and supporting the retention and expansion of existing enterprises to provide high-wage job opportunities.

Become An Investor

Becoming an investor in the Lee’s Summit Economic Development Council is an opportunity to help shape the future of our community. Investors partner to drive sustainable growth, support business retention and expansion, and attract new industries that create high-quality jobs. Your investment strengthens collaboration between business, education, and civic leadership, ensuring Lee’s Summit continues to thrive as a vibrant, forward-looking city.

Become A Friend

Becoming a Friend of the Lee’s Summit Economic Development Council is a great way for young professionals and small businesses to get involved in shaping our city’s future. Friends connect with local leaders, expand their networks, and support efforts that grow and strengthen our business community. It’s an accessible entry point to make a real impact while building lasting relationships. We ask that all Friend level entities already be a member of the Lee's Summit Chamber or Downtown Lee's Summit Main Street.

Lee’s Summit is one of Missouri’s fastest-growing cities—and it’s no coincidence. Our progress is driven by a community that shows up, builds up, and steps up. From award-winning schools and a skilled workforce to visionary leadership and strategic development, Lee’s Summit is where businesses thrive and families plant roots.

Education & Healthcare

Home to award-winning K–12 schools, Longview Community College, and a University of Central Missouri campus, plus three critical care hospitals and a robust healthcare network.

Growth & Prosperity

Lee’s Summit grew from 71,564 residents in 2000 to 107,281 in 2024  with a household income of $104,989 and a 74% homeownership rate.

Sports & Recreation

Featuring top facilities like TM Fieldhouse, Paragon Star Soccer Complex, and Summit Waves, along with the Tour de Lakes bike ride and over 100 miles of trails for year-round activity.

Entertainment & Culture

From Legacy Park Amphitheater and the Lee’s Summit Symphony to downtown festivals and Green Street, there’s no shortage of local culture and entertainment.
