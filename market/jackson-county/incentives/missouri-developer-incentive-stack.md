## Mandatory pre-read: The Threshold Rule

The ED Incentive Policy has a **strict threshold rule** the policy calls out:

> *"If request is below the listed minimum investment, City staff may proceed with review and presentation to Council. If request exceeds listed amount, a conceptual presentation shall first be made to the City Council before proceeding with request."*

In plain terms: **small projects get accelerated review; large projects require a Council concept presentation first.** This is the most important procedural lever to understand.

## Statutory Financial Tools (Missouri State Law)

| Tool | Min Threshold | Cap / Term | Use Case |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tax Increment Financing (TIF)** | $5M new / $3M existing | up to 25% of private development cost | Reimburses grading, utility installation, public road widening |
| **Community Improvement District (CID)** | Public improvement required | up to 1¢ sales tax | Ongoing security, landscaping, private parking maintenance |
| **Neighborhood Improvement District (NID)** | Public improvement required | Special assessment | Neighborhood-scale public improvements |
| **Transportation Development District (TDD)** | Public improvement required | up to 1¢ sales tax | Traffic interchanges, signalization, transit turnouts |
| **Chapter 100 IRBs** | $5M new / $3M existing | 50% - 10 yrs | Industrial Revenue Bonds — title held by City during dev |
| **Chapter 353** | $5M new / $3M existing | 50% - 10 yrs | Urban Redevelopment Corp |
| **Land Clearance for Redevelopment Authority (LCRA)** | $500K | 50% - 10 yrs | Redevelopment of blighted areas |
| **Sales Tax Reimbursement** | $250K (public improvements) | up to ½¢ | Project-specific public improvements |
| **Site-Specific Incentives** | All categories | TBD | Bespoke negotiation |

## The Big Three: Hoizontal Relief for Veritas

### 1. Chapter 353 Redevelopment (Top Tier for Veritas)

> **"Up to 75% tax abatement over the first 10 years, and up to 37.5% abatement over the subsequent 15 years."**

**Structuring:** Eligible developers receive statutory property tax relief specifically designed to clear blighted or underutilized zones.

**Veritas fit:** Strong if Prime Lee's Summit land has any blight classification (it doesn't, but adjacent parcels may). The 25-year abatement horizon is a major proforma lever.

### 2. Chapter 100 Industrial Revenue Bonds (IRBs)

> **"Allows the city to take structural title of a facility during development, rendering the construction phase 100% exempt from real estate and personal sales tax on building materials."**

**Structuring:** Municipal bond issuance; private developer benefits from tax-exempt construction.

**Veritas fit:** Direct fit for any industrial, manufacturing, or large-scale commercial project. **100% construction-phase tax exemption** is the cleanest cost reduction available.

### 3. Tax Increment Financing (TIF)

> **"Captures the incremental increase in property and local sales taxes generated by a new development over a maximum of [term] to directly reimburse developers for grading, utility installation, and public road widening."**

**Veritas fit:** Direct match for Veritas's site-prep + grading + utility work — those are exactly the line items TIF reimburses. **This is the most relevant incentive for ground-up development.**

## Detailed Provisions (from Ord. #9723)

### Targeted Outcomes (City seeks investment that...)

1. **Develops/maintains infrastructure** — bridges, streets, signals, stormwater, water/sewer
2. **Attracts/retains targeted businesses** — leverage existing strengths (workforce, education)
3. **Preserves/enhances residential developments** — quality design, mixed types, accessibility
4. **Encourages business retention/expansion** — partnerships with existing employers
5. **Generates positive community image** — meets/exceeds UDO design requirements

### Targeted Areas (Priority Locations)

The City has defined priority zones — projects here get priority review:

| Targeted Planning Area | Description |
|---|---|
| Douglas/Tudor Rd | Union Pacific rail line bisects; large undeveloped tracts; mixed-use vision |
| I-470 North | 1,100 acres (Property Reserve, Inc.); Ralph Powell corridor opportunities |
| Airport | City-owned; 5,500 ft runway; I-470 corridor development |
| I-470 Chipman/Colbern | Land reclamation; Pryor Rd potential; Conceptual Dev Plan in progress |
| View High Corridor | Paragon Star + Village at View High; Longview Farm redevelopment |
| Downtown Core | Multiple redevelopment parcels; former Post Office → coworking |
| M-291 North | At-Home, Hy-Vee expansion, Generation 3 QuikTrip, PetSmart |
| US 50 Hwy | Blackwell interchange complete; The Grove mixed-use opportunity |
| South M-291 | Pine Tree Plaza redeveloped; The Grove; Wal-Mart (March 2017) |

### Strategic Direction (Vision)

> *"Lee's Summit will build upon and promote its unique downtown, educational excellence and cultural heritage to create and nurture a business environment which fosters entrepreneurship, commercial and neighborhood redevelopment, and the attraction and retention of high-quality jobs in targeted businesses."*

Three strategic pillars:
- **Strategic Economic Development** — one of 7 goals of Ignite! Comprehensive Plan
- **Diversified Economic Base** — broad sector appeal
- **Fiscal Sustainability** — tax base must grow

## LSEDC Direct Investor Tiers (Community Access)

Separate from city/municipal incentives, **LSEDC** offers tiered investor benefits for direct community access:

| Tier | Annual Levels | Key Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| **Friend** | $150-500 | Annual report, quarterly luncheons, networking. Requires Chamber/Main Street membership |
| **Patron** | $1,250 | All Friend + annual 1-on-1 with leadership |
| **Benefactor** | $2,750 | All Patron + LSEDC Preferred Vendor List |
| **Leadership** | $4,950 | All Benefactor + Investor Spotlight |
| **Partner** | $7,200 | All Leadership + Featured Social Media |
| **Pacesetter** | $8,000 | All Partner + 50% off event sponsorships |
| **Grand** | $10,000+ | All Pacesetter + Newsletter Spotlight + By Agreement |

**Veritas fit:** Joining at **Patron level ($1,250)** would unlock direct 1-on-1 with LSEDC leadership — useful for capital-raise positioning and developer relationships.

| Utility installation | $TBD | TIF / NID | Reimbursement |
| Road widening | $TBD | TDD / TIF | Up to 1¢ sales tax capture |

**Net effect:** First-decade property tax burden can be reduced by 75% + construction-phase tax exemption. **This is the single most-important capital stack lever for Veritas Prime Lee's Summit.**

## Application Process

1. **Pre-application consultation** with LSEDC staff
2. **Financial Incentive Application Worksheet** (formal request)
3. **Concept presentation to Council** (if above threshold)
4. **Review by Economic Development Council** (Chapter 353, TIF commissions)
5. **Approval process** — varies by tool

## Strategic Recommendations for Veritas

1. **Engage LSEDC early** — Patron-tier membership + pre-application consultation
2. **Stack multiple tools** — TIF + Chapter 100 + Chapter 353 are compatible
3. **Target the targeted areas** — Douglas/Tudor, US 50, South M-291 are priority
4. **Lead with infrastructure benefit** — site's utility/road work is the best TIF case
5. **Show density vs. infrastructure mitigation** — community sentiment is now sensitive to this
