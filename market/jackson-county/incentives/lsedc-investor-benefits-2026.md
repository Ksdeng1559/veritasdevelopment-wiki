## Veritas Strategic Recommendation

### Tier recommendation: **Patron ($1,250/year)**

**Why Patron specifically:**
- $1,250 unlocks **annual 1-on-1 with LSEDC leadership** — direct access to insider timing
- Affordable for first-year engagement
- 3-year prepay drops to $1,125/year (saves $375)
- 3 years of patronage → Veritas becomes Board-eligible (governance seat)
- Costs roughly the same as 1 day of attorney time — high ROI

### Engagement Sequence

1. **Month 1:** Join at Patron level (smallest "real investor" tier)
2. **Month 2:** First quarterly luncheon (network with developers, banks, brokers)
3. **Month 3:** Annual 1-on-1 with leadership (introduce Veritas + Prime Lee's Summit)
4. **Month 6:** Apply for pre-application consultation on tax incentives
5. **Month 12:** Re-up; consider Benefactor tier if engagement is yielding results
6. **Year 3:** Board-eligible; pursue seat
