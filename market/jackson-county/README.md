### The developer opportunity stack

1. **4,000-acre master corridor expansion** — church-owned tracts being deployed into integrated high-density master-planned community nodes
2. **Missouri statutory tax tools** — Chapter 353 (75% abatement first 10 years), Chapter 100 IRBs (100% tax-exempt construction), TIF, CID, TDD
3. **LSEDC direct investor access** — tiered investment levels ($150-$10,000+) + 1-on-1 with leadership
4. **Top-tier workforce pipeline** — 905,800 workers within 45-min drive; 80% commute <24 miles
5. **Pro-development community sentiment** — "Costco Effect" + 39% population growth in 24 years

### The friction points to navigate

1. **Developer incentive scrutiny** — high-value tax abatements draw critique
2. **Density vs. infrastructure strain** — high-density projects meet resistance
3. **School capacity concerns** — R-7 school district crowding
4. **Protest petitions** — 13 on senior community CCRC (validated 2026)
