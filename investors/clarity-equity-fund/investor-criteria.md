## Investor Criteria (from Seth Wilson's LinkedIn fund-launch post)

### Targeted Returns
- **Minimum gross 2.0x Equity Multiple (EM)** — this is the hard floor for any deal they consider

### Structure
- **Semi-liquid**: semi-annual liquidity windows (vs. traditional 10-year lock-ups)

### AI-Driven Precision
- Uses **"David"**, a proprietary AI forensic underwriting agent
- Analyzes deal velocity, verifies data, mitigates risk with institutional-grade accuracy

### Strategic Niche
- Focuses on the **middle market** — the gap between friends-and-family raises and large institutional capital

### Sponsor/Project Requirements
- **Avoids speculative land acquisition**
- Focuses only on **"shovel-ready" projects** and **"value-add" opportunities with established income streams**

### Investor Benefits (what they promise LPs)
1. **Institutional governance** — world-class vendors for audit, finance, legal, fund administration
2. **Tax efficiency** — aggressive cost segregation + depreciation pass-throughs
3. **Alignment of interests** — low, transparent fees; GP only succeeds when investors do
4. **Risk mitigation** — no speculative land; shovel-ready + value-add only

### Target LPs
- Family offices
- Ultra-high-net-worth individuals (UHNWI)
- Registered Investment Advisors (RIAs)
- "Enterprising" RIAs

### Bottom-line fit assessment
**Clarity is NOT a direct fit for Veritas's current Prime Lee's Summit raise** — they specifically avoid speculative land and non-shovel-ready projects. But:

1. **Bridge opportunity**: If Veritas could position a *later phase* (post-entitlement, pre-construction = shovel-ready), Clarity becomes viable
2. **Geography overlap**: KS/MO matches — Seth Wilson's KC base makes in-person pitch natural
3. **JV fit**: Clarity's semi-liquid structure + family-office LP base could fit a *post-seed* JV tranche in Veritas's staged capital model
4. **Adjacent opportunity**: Build-to-rent search is active in KS/MO — if Veritas pivots to BTR, Clarity is a relevant capital partner
